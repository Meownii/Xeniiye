HOME SITE PREVIEW

To preview: extract the ZIP, open the site-rascunho folder, then open index.html.

The visual-novel introduction opens first. Click anywhere in the intro to finish
the current typewriter line or advance to the next one. Skip and Replay are available.
Replace the character art, character name, and dialogue placeholders with the
client's approved assets and text.

The navigation opens Home, About Me, Commissions, Shop, Terms of Service, Contact, and an optional Feedback page. Text, product details, prices, links, and commission policies are
placeholders until the client provides the approved information. No prices,
contact details, or purchase URLs have been invented.

Banner, portrait, commission, and product art areas have larger proportions so
future images can display with more detail. Replace each marked image area with
the corresponding client-provided art.

The music panel is a visual demo with an animated disc and equalizer. Its pause
button pauses the animation. No audio file is included yet.

The compact system bar has a live clock. The clock widget also has analog hands
and a working 12-hour / 24-hour display switch.

The site keeps its approved layout with a Frutiger Aero-inspired palette:
bright blue glass surfaces and coral-red, yellow, and green accents. The logo
has been removed from the header. Panels and image slots use varied, rounded
silhouettes to make the dashboard feel less rigid.
The subtle blue checkerboard remains outside the main window. Section layouts stay
text-led without unrelated decorative photos. The profile rail has a retro phone
with a placeholder for the client-provided character image. The banner stays
inside its frame on narrow screens, and the introduction dialog is centered on
the viewport.

The Feedback page is only a visual placeholder. A form and any submission
service should be added only if the client approves the feature.
